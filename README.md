# 🌙 DeenHistory

**DeenHistory** is a comprehensive, interactive Islamic history and divine scripture web application built to make learning Islamic history, Seerah, and the Holy Quran accessible, structured, and engaging.

## ✨ Features
* **The Holy Quran:** Read with Uthmani script, English translation, and listen to verse-by-verse audio recitations (by Mishary Rashid Alafasy) with full Surah queue playback.
* **Prophets & Seerah:** Detailed chronological logs of the 25 Prophets mentioned in the Quran alongside milestone epochs of Prophet Muhammad's (ﷺ) life.
* **Companions & Eras:** Comprehensive records of the Sahabah, major historical battles, and golden-age Islamic caliphates.
* **Signposts & Lessons:** Explore prophetical accounts of major signs of Judgment Day and lessons from historical civilizations.
* **Android & Web Ready:** Available as a responsive web app and downloadable Android APK.
