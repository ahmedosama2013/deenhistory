Information I Do Not Collect
  • No accounts, sign-ups, or passwords.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment details.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • No precise location data, contacts, photos, or files from your device.
Information Stored On Your Device

The app saves your light/dark theme choice in your browser's or device's local storage so it can be remembered next time. This data stays on your device, is never sent to me, and can be cleared at any time through your browser or app settings.

Third-Party Services

To provide certain features, the app connects to third-party services. When you use these features, your device contacts those services directly, and they may receive technical information such as your IP address and device/browser type under their own privacy policies:

  • Al-Quran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud): supplies the Quran text, English translation, and recitation audio listings shown in The Holy Quran section.
  • Quran audio hosting: the recitation audio files (Mishary Rashid Alafasy) are streamed from the audio host provided by the API.
Search

The search bar works entirely on your device across the content already loaded in the app. Your search queries are not sent to us or stored.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to delete or share.

Data Security

Since I do not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no user database that could be exposed. Content is delivered over standard web connections, and third-party services are responsible for the security of their own systems.
